# Landing page (#welcome)

Scope: the in-app landing page opened by the nav logo. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: portfolio viewers judging the product's craft. Job: understand what CineTrack is within seconds, believe it is thoughtfully made, then step into the app.
Content: neutral product content (TMDb trending artwork, labelled sample data); no personal library, no invented claims, TMDb attribution required.
Lead points: proper TV tracking, discovery, viewing history.
Constraints: inherit the app's world exactly (Paper/Noir tokens, Instrument Serif display, Inter, small tracked labels, 2px corners, crimson accent); works in both themes; reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a film's opening title sequence: one credit card per screen, set in the app's own type, each naming a capability and proving it with a working fragment. It refuses the split hero with a feature-card row.

OWN-WORLD: The app's Paper or Noir ground, Instrument Serif at title-card scale with the accent italic, small tracked uppercase role labels, dotted leaders, hairline rules, crimson only for the accent word and the primary action.

STORY: The visitor reads the main title and the one-line offer, then scrolls through three credits (episodes, discovery, a year in film), each with something to touch, then the end-credit roll lists every capability and the data credit, and they enter the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred title card, no label above it: "CineTrack" at title-card scale with "Cine" in accent italic, a one-line offer under it, then two actions on one line: primary "Open your library", secondary "Discover something". A strip of trending TMDb posters sits low in the frame like the opening shot behind the titles, with a scroll cue.
